2025 Cigarette 41 Nighthawk

The innovative 41’ Nighthawk is designed to let you cruise the water in true Cigarette style. The Nighthawk offers the latest Cigarette Racing design, with its all new hard top. Loaded with state-of-the-art technology, the Nighthawk is as luxurious as it is thrilling to drive. Cigarette proves yet again that high performance and comfort do not have to be mutually exclusive. The 41’ Nighthawk provides direct, unadulterated, performance combined with comfort that is like no other center console.

  • Length: 41’
  • Beam: 11’
  • Fuel Type: Gas
