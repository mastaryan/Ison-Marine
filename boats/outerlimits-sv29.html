Outerlimits SV29

This is a true one-off build by Eliminator of the legendary Outerlimits SV29. It has won several awards, including best paint and graphics, which was presented by the poker run legend, Stu Jones with FPC. It was featured in an article by Matt Trulio, with Speed on the water. A true show stopper. All get coat.
The boat is outfitted with a Mercury Racing 600SCI. When we purchased the boat it had a 4 blade prop and would run about 100 MPH. Since then we have installed a Mercury Racing Labbed 34p 5-blade prop, which slows it to 90+, loaded with fuel and crew, but it takes off and handles perfectly. Lavorsi gauges, 380 k plane tabs, upgraded 10” vessel view with charts. Cockpit cover, full cover, XR ITS Sportsmaster, fusion/kicker stereo, 300 hours on factory sealed engine.
